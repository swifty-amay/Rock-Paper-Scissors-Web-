# Rock, Paper & Scissor
A classic yet famous game which almost everyone of us had played during our childhood. And even now many of you might have been playing it with your friends when you have to select anything or come to a conclusion or select anything among yourselves. So here it is, a web version of it. The only difference here is that here you will be playing with the computer as your friend 😅.

## How to play?
So the game is simple and easy to play. You will be shown 3 buttons for each choice i.e rock, paper and scissor. After your selection you will be shown your score against the computer's choice and after 5 rounds of play you will be shown your final score. And that's it! Easy and simple to play. So hope you'll enjoy the game and feel free to explore the codebase if you want to see the game logic. You'll find it in **<u>[game.js](https://github.com/swifty-amay/Rock-Paper-Scissors-Web-/blob/main/game.js)</u>** file.

## Concepts Used
Well to be very precised, there's nothing much other than javascript logic in this game. So here are the topics or thins you'll see in the javascript file:-
- conditional statements (if, else if & else)
- switch case statements.
- how to create and use functions.
- DOM Manipulation
- Event Listeners for buttons.
